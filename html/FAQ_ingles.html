Frequently Asked Questions

  • What are the general office hours?

    Outpatient consultations: Monday to Friday from 8:00 to 17:00. On-call and emergency care: 24 hours every day.

  • How do I request an appointment for a specialty?

    You can request an appointment by phone through the central scheduling number, via the website form, or in person at admission. Please bring your ID and your health insurance or affiliate number.

  • What should I do in case of an emergency?

    If it is a life-threatening emergency, go directly to the nearest emergency department or call the local emergency number. Emergency care is prioritized by severity.

  • Do you accept health insurance and prepaid plans?

    The hospital works with most health insurance providers and private plans. Please check admission or your insurer for coverage and referrals.

  • How can I obtain test results?

    Results are delivered in the reports area; some studies can be viewed online if you registered in the patient system. For urgent deliveries, please contact your physician.

  • What documents should I bring for scheduled hospitalization?

    ID, health insurance card or prepaid plan information, prior studies requested by the medical team, and a list of current medications. Please consult the information sheet provided by the service.

  • How can I request a transfer between hospitals?

    Transfers are coordinated through the medical director or admission. In case of discharge with a scheduled transfer, the social work service manages the logistics and transportation.
